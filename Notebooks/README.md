This folder contains a compact version of the notebooks used to reproduce the Kinbiont analyses of the paper.

- **CoC_decision_tree.ipynb**: A decision tree example of the extended data figure, used to study the ecological interactions of *Drosophila* gut microbiome isolates.  
- **DT_N_soli.ipynb**: The decision tree analysis of the results for *N. soli* in Figures 5 and 6.  
- **EtOH_bioproduction.ipynb**: The fits of multidimensional data from Figure 3.  
- **Fitting_diauxic_and_phages_data.ipynb**: The fits of diauxic growth and *E. coli* infected with phages from Figure 3.  
- **Monod_experiment_symbolic_regression.ipynb**: The inference of growth rate empirical laws using symbolic regression,  nutrient limitation case of study.
- **CHL_dose_resoponse.ipynb**: The inference of growth rate empirical laws using symbolic regression, antibiotic dose-response case of study.
